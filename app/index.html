      What do these numbers mean?

      They are illustrative model indices, not biomass measurements. Grass share is a simple proxy for grass food. Tree pixels don’t measure edible shrubs, so set shrub food yourself. Water and trees are context only: they don’t move the creeks or change species rules. “No pixels found” isn’t proof something is absent.
