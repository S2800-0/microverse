BEHIND THE WORLD

Even this little world
has a footprint.

We reuse generated artwork and run movement and ecosystem experiments without fresh AI calls. Computing still uses resources, and we haven’t measured this project’s full environmental footprint.

A little AI. A lot of reusable craft.

Muse Image creates optional illustrations. SAM makes cutouts. Spark helps interpret custom questions. Our code runs the ecosystem, movement, validated changes, and replay.

Prepared questions work without model inference. Voice and custom creation remain optional.

What could others build with this?

Reusable creature puppets, experiments you can rerun, and explanations tied to recorded events. These are building blocks to explore, rather than demonstrated educational or conservation outcomes.

Progress, trade-offs, and sources

Meta reports improvements to cooling and has water-restoration and clean-energy goals. We haven’t established that Muse is environmentally better than another provider or measured this app’s water use.
