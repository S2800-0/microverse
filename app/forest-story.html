About this forest story

This focused view shows one rabbit; all the other creatures still run in both model forests. It is a selective illustration, not a view for counting animals. Painted grass strokes illustrate the model’s food scale. They are not measured plants. The rabbit’s whole-image hop is an artistic interpretation.
