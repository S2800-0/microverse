ABOUT THIS LITTLE WORLD

Your photos and questions

The forest simulation and prepared stories run on your device. No account is needed to play.

Optional AI

Only choose AI features with photos and recordings you are allowed to send. Vercel forwards optional AI requests to our Netlify backend. A creature or place photo then goes to Meta. A spoken question goes to Meta for transcription. Custom reasoning uses the funded provider named in the interface, usually Baseten’s Muse Spark Open. Questions and model evidence are sent; creature pictures, names and learner notes are stripped from reasoning replays. Do not put private information in a question.

While a job is queued, its processed input is encrypted in temporary backend storage. After processing, that input is replaced by an encrypted result. Results expire after 15 minutes; hourly cleanup removes expired jobs. Provider data handling is governed by the provider’s terms; this app cannot erase a request already sent. Closing a job stops waiting for its result, and does not cancel or refund work already sent.

Saved work

Your creature collection stays in this browser when you choose Save. Downloaded discovery cards and experiments stay on your device. A discovery preview URL can contain your responses in its browser fragment; keep that URL private. Some experiment downloads can include creature pictures. No public gallery or learner-response upload is created.

Fair access

A signed first-party cookie identifies your temporary AI session for one day. Server-side keyed hashes limit requests by session and network; raw IP addresses are not saved in the app’s spending ledger. Vercel, Netlify and AI providers may retain infrastructure logs under their own policies. The app does not log photos, recordings or questions.

This is a hackathon prototype with a small shared AI test allowance. Prepared examples continue working when the allowance is exhausted.
