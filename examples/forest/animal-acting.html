What this study does—and what still needs review

It reuses the saved fox’s 24 locally rendered poses. Frame choice follows travel distance. Each pose is aligned to its visible foot line; the contact shadow stays on the ground. Turning uses a brief visual pivot and stopping returns to rest. It is an artistic 2D cutout, not reconstructed animal anatomy or ecological behaviour.

The rabbit in the lesson uses whole-image hops. Deer articulation remains under review and is not demonstrated as fixed here. No new artwork or paid calls are involved.

The sample timing reports local drawing work only. It is not a cross-device performance benchmark.
