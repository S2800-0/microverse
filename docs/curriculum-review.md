# Initial curriculum correspondence check

This is a developer review of selected published learning goals against the implemented model and activity, dated October 27, 2026. It is not a teacher review, an exhaustive textbook evaluation, a standards certification or evidence of learning gains. No participant sessions have been recorded.

Sources inspected:

- [OpenSciEd 7.5 Ecosystem Dynamics](https://openscied.org/instructional-materials/7-5-ecosystem-dynamics/): resource availability, interactions, disruptions, evidence and investigation. Its orangutan/farming curriculum includes social and ecological content this forest does not implement.
- [Siyavula Grade 8, Interactions and interdependence](https://www.siyavula.com/read/za/natural-sciences/grade-8/interactions-and-interdependence-within-the-environment): ecology, feeding relationships and food webs. A chapter heading is not proof that this app covers the whole topic.
- [NGSS MS-LS2-4](https://www.nextgenscience.org/pe/ms-ls2-4-ecosystems-interactions-energy-and-dynamics): arguments about ecosystem changes supported by empirical evidence. Our records are simulation evidence; field/literature evidence is still required for claims about real ecosystems.

| Selected goal | Check performed | Finding and boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Investigate resource availability | Executed half-rainfall experiments and inspected regrowth rules. At default week 1, grass index was about 78.0/100 in the reference and 73.4/100 with reduced rainfall; initial animal counts still matched. | Supports observing an early resource difference. Growth constants are tuned, not calibrated to a real site. |
| Trace indirect feeding connections | Inspected explicit rabbit → grass, deer → browse, fox → rabbit, wolf → deer/rabbit rules and their energy/breeding links. | Supports a small, stated food web. Does not model decomposers, pollination or complete energy/nutrient cycling. |
| Distinguish a recorded difference from an explanation | Inspected weeks 13 and 52 and five future-randomness continuations at week 52. Default rabbit counts were 40/37 at week 13 and 9/13 at week 52 (reference/reduced rain); continuation differences were mixed. | The activity must not teach that lower rainfall necessarily yields fewer rabbits in every run. Population accounting identifies births/deaths/arrivals; it does not establish one biological cause or validate the model's realism. |
| Compare and inspect evidence | Checked same-snapshot paired runs, exact event references, post-start filtering and population reconciliation. | Supports within-model comparison and replay. Random streams can diverge; broad causal inference and independent empirical validation remain outside this check. |
| Reflect on a familiar environment | Inspected the prediction, learner-first explanation, everyday connection and explicit outdoor-observation question. | Provides a discussion prompt. Attitude change, transfer and real environmental actions have not been measured. |

The same default food difference remained negative in five checked random continuations at week 52. This small check is not a robustness benchmark or ecological calibration. Do not use it to select only favourable animal outcomes for a demo.

Next evaluation: identify one lesson objective with a teacher/facilitator, observe a real attempt, record assistance/confusion and revisit whether the model helps the intended explanation. If no participant is available before submission, report this correspondence check and developer walkthrough as completed, and leave participant usability/learning evaluation as future work.
