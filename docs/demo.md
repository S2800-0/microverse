# Short classroom demo and remaining evidence

## Three-minute spine

1. Open the forest paused. Bring in an owned/cleared pet photo or a saved cutout. Explain the explicit companion role; artwork does not identify species biology. Review/fallback before adding. A saved processed PNG makes this part repeatable without inference.
2. Open Place photo and choose the **recorded** garden example. Say that it is a saved SAM run, not a live camera result. Inspect a coloured mask and its unknown pixels. Review the shares, then deliberately choose model grass/shrub food. Explain the illustrative proxy and fixed map.
3. Ask a prepared question for a dependable no-inference path, or explicitly ask funded Spark to translate a custom rainfall/removal question. Read the exact change and predict an outcome before running. Unsupported questions must clarify/reject rather than invent rules.
4. Advance both worlds 13 weeks. Point to retained creature identity, food/population differences and one event reference. Write a learner explanation first, then open the local evidence guide or ask funded Spark to select IDs. The application renders the counts/events; model mechanisms are possible connections, not proven causes.
5. Open a saved experiment and show the same starting inputs, image/rig, notes and reproduced results. The worksheet/model-only rerun removes creature pictures and learner notes from the embedded rerun; the full replay can contain personal imagery. Discuss what outdoor evidence a real ecological claim would need.

Keep the 15–20 minute [teacher activity](classroom.md) as the actual lesson. The place segment is an optional model-critique prompt, not a measured improvement to that lesson. Microphone input and neural speech are optional accessibility/usability features; typing and captions remain available. A static/offline-capable sample path is a fallback with explicit source labels, not a concealed live-model claim.
