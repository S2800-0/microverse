# V11 child-style journeys and live spending proposal

3 November 2026. These are agent-run scenarios, not findings from a real child or teacher.

## No-cost scripted checks

- First visit: notice camera/library/craft/tools; choose guide; identify water using explicit Yes / Not this area choices; skip uncertainty without silently accepting a mask.
- Garden and woodland: compare wide and close views and food previews. Keep the place visually recognizable; distinguish shared teaching rules from an independently validated biome.
- Fish: watch a complete approach → bite → departure → regrowth cycle, pause, seven-day step, reduced motion, close-up and phone controls. Feeding must consume a particular patch; overlays must reference an actual record.
- Rabbit: no teleporting focus camera or off-map borders while zoomed. Show where food was before/after; hints name the actual button to press.
- Spark: common question, custom question, unsupported request, empty input, consent declined, allowance exhausted, interrupted response. Companion/hint speaking state follows actual speech playback.
- Upload: garden, woodland, provided pet photo and an ordinary non-landscape object fixture; decode failure, absent clues and imagined-world routing. No unconsented upload or keys in client assets.

The original total $1.10 Meta test allowance is fully reserved. Proposed new total: $1.70, an additional $0.60 maximum, at $0.05 conservative reservation per provider operation. No automatic retry; stop at the cap; reuse cached successes.

- Two complete photo-world pipelines with visually different local garden/woodland sources: plan, ground, sprite sheet, optional bounded motion analysis. At most eight operations ($0.40 reserved).
- Two custom Spark questions against actual replay: a diet question and an unsupported request. At most two operations ($0.10 reserved).
- Two operations maximum held for one alternate non-landscape plan or one failed request, not silently spent on retries ($0.10 reserved).

The live fixtures use already local public/example photos. Do not send a private pet/photo without its explicit consent path. No AWS/Baseten calls. Reservations remain bookkeeping, not billing proof. New live artwork can replace the similar meadow paintings only after visual review.
